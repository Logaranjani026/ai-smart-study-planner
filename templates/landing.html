Intelligent Recommendation Core

How Our AI Prioritizes Your Study Hours

Instead of generic to-do lists, our Python recommendation engine computes a dynamic priority score for every pending topic using exam urgency, difficulty weight, weakness severity, and forgetting-curve decay.

  • 1 Exam Proximity Weighting: Automatically boosts subjects with approaching exam dates while keeping foundation courses steady.
  • 2 Weakness-Aware Allocation: Allocates deeper blocks and spaced revision slots to topics flagged by low quiz scores or confidence ratings.
  • 3 Real-Time Adaptation: Logs every completed or missed session to MySQL and re-balances tomorrow's plan automatically.
